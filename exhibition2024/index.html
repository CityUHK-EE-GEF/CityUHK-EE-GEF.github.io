Project ID : P09
DETECTING HIGH STRESS LEVELS AND ANXIETY IN CLASSROOMS
Project InformationPoster
P09
DETECTING HIGH STRESS LEVELS AND ANXIETY IN CLASSROOMS
Student Names:
Anson Hung, Cruz Wong, Lester Li, Jessie Yim
Video Demo
GitHub
Anxiety and high stress levels are prevalent in urban environments, just like Hong Kong. Statistics show that 44.7% of Hong Kong university students face anxiety issues. Every year, 120,000 lives are lost to overwork and stress. Stress that festers into heart disease, strokes, and accidents. Students are sacrificing sleep, friendships, and joy due to high stress. Within classrooms, students are unable to focus, worrying about upcoming deadlines and the vast amounts of extracurricular activities that they need to complete. Students overwhelmed in class usually daydream (overthink), or fidget, and lose attention within class. Teachers who don't understand the underlying issue of the students usually scold students for natural responses to high-stress environments. Students’ anxiety levels usually grow higher due to the overreaction of teachers. Students want to be heard and understood; therefore, allowing teachers to recognise issues in the first place is very important.

Assistive Communication Device for Speech Impairments
